# Facilitator session script

Copy the session template in [feedback.md](feedback.md) once per participant. Record the deployed build, device/browser/version and role. Do not fill outcomes until observed.

## Opening — 2 minutes

Explain that this is an experimental devnet app using test tokens, participation is voluntary, and notes are anonymous. Confirm the participant agrees to notes and that their test wallet has the required test tokens. Ask: “What do you think this site lets you do?” Do not explain the interface in advance.

## Creator tasks — approximately 15 minutes

1. “Create your own VYNX page with an available alias.” Watch whether the landing page prompts a wallet before an intentional action, whether the participant understands connecting versus signing in, and whether they can identify the network and cost before approving. Record the final alias and whether registration is verified. If a payment was sent but confirmation is unclear, keep the signature and use verification recovery.
2. “Add a display name, short description, image and link. Publish the page, then reload to check your changes.” Observe image/input errors and whether saving locally versus publishing is understood. Ask the participant to describe what is public.
3. “Share the page with your paired supporter and find out whether their tip arrived.” Record the tip currency, amount and verified dashboard state. Do not assume an explorer transaction means application verification succeeded.
4. “Hide your page, confirm what another visitor can see, then publish it again.” Explain the public-image URL limitation only after observing their expectation; record the explanation as facilitator help. Use a fresh anonymous browser to check the page. Separately published sponsorship offers are a separate surface.
5. “Sign out, then try to open the editor again.” Observe whether the signed-in session is revoked and the participant understands how to return.

## Supporter tasks — approximately 10 minutes

1. “Open your paired creator’s page. Find what they make and how to support them.” Confirm navigation on the actual viewport and whether the network/test-token notice is understood.
2. “Start a tip, then cancel the wallet approval once. Try again when you’re ready.” Record the visible cancellation message, whether any transaction was sent and whether the retry is understandable.
3. “Send a small devnet USDC tip and confirm it completed.” Use an amount within the displayed allowed range. Check the recipient, amount, network and fee payer before signing. If USDC sponsorship is unavailable, record the failure and switch to a small devnet SOL tip with enough SOL for its fee; leave USDC coverage unpassed.
4. “Find the payment reference and what you would do if it were still pending.” Do not deliberately sabotage a real payment. Record whether they discover verification recovery and support without coaching. Check that the creator sees the same verified tip.

## Optional sponsorship follow-up

Only after the core flow: a creator publishes an offer, a supporter submits a proposal, the creator approves it, and the supporter sends the agreed test-token payment. Verify the ad and displayed publication period. Record whether the participant understands direct payment, no escrow and no guaranteed traffic. Mark this flow not attempted if time, approval or test funds are unavailable.

## Closing — 3 minutes

Ask these questions and record the actual answers:

- “Where did you get stuck or need help?”
- “What did you think signing would do?”
- “Which tokens, network and fees did you think you were using?”
- “If a payment was pending, what would you do next?”
- “Would anything stop you from using this again?”

For each reported blocker, ask for the task, expected result, actual result and visible error. Do not ask for private keys, recovery phrases, full sensitive payloads or session cookies. Ask permission before saving a participant-supplied screenshot and redact unrelated personal information.
